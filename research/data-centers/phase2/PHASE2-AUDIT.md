### Manually audited examples and traps

1. **SG6:** the undated timeline saysQ1 2027, while the dated6 November2026 [announcement](https://investor.equinix.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1130/equinix-opens-sg6-in-singapore-expanding-ai-ready-capacity) explicitly reports opening. Status follows the dated announcement; the conflicting target is held. First-phase8MW is distinct from full-buildout20MW. Initial investment is reported as `$290m`; the currency is left unresolved rather than inferred silently.
2. **Osaka5x-1:** Q1 2027 is accompanied by “timing under assessment”; it is not a firm commitment. USD177m phase cost /19MW phase capacity are not whole-campus figures. Portfolio phase values and qualifiers are kept.
3. **DE3:** [current facility specification](https://www.coresite.com/data-center/de3-denver-co) gives **18CMW** for the first building and a2026 availability target;60CMW is campus-wide. The earlier [announcement](https://www.coresite.com/news/coresite-achieves-key-construction-milestone-for-new-de3-data-center-in-denver) says180,000sqft; the current page says about170,000. Both area claims remain, with no resolved single area. No opening is invented from the2026 target. Nearby DE1/DE2 connectivity is linked only because the official text documents it, never by distance.
4. **Polaris Forge1:** campus contracted400MW; Building2 is150MW delivered in two75MW phases; Building3 is150MW. The campus overview is excluded from project totals when its phases are represented. Phase2 delivery is1 October2026, not the fiscal-quarter end or retrieval date.
5. **APLD Building3:** `$1.59bn` notes also repay a`$300m` bridge. Note proceeds are financing, **not project cost**. Unknown currency is not silently converted.
6. **Delta Forge2:**210MW/15-year lease and`$5.2bn` base-term contracted revenue are not capex or property value. Unnamed hyperscaler tenants stay unnamed. Only Polaris Forge1 is explicitly linked to CoreWeave as tenant, not property owner. CoreWeave's existing registry ID is retained as a tenant reference, but no unverified investment route is generated for it.
7. **DLR/Mitsubishi:** current disclosure supports20% DLR/80% Mitsubishi in the named Dallas JV after the2025 contribution; old35% DLR must not survive. It is a JV-level share, not a percentage copied to every Dallas facility. BDIA Jakarta50% and32MW refer to a named JV/two buildings/two parcels at the older2025 disclosure date; current facility allocations are not established.
8. **NY2/NY3:** the official source names two facilities at the same stated address. An address alone must not collapse distinct buildings/phases. Conversely, the dataset does not count arbitrary campus/programme rows as separate construction sites.

## Licensing / public eligibility

**Zero public approvals.** Corporate directories, presentations and announcements are internal factual research sources here, not automatically licensed feeds. The previously reviewed Equinix/DLR copying/republication restrictions remain. Corporate press-release accessibility does not by itself create a commercial republication grant. SEC-hosted issuer text is not treated as a blanket US-government-public-domain work. Only minimal extracted facts/claims, source pointers and hashes are versioned; full downloaded corporate/SEC bodies/PDFs stay locally ignored.

The release blocker is a documented permission or reviewed lawful basis for the particular narrowly selected factual reuse, plus source-specific contractual/database/image obligations. An owner preference alone cannot grant third-party rights. No public ingest or shared adapter is included. The exact-location/source gaps also block globe release even if a licence were cleared.
