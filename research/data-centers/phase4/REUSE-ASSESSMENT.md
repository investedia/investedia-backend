# Phase 4 — source and field reuse assessment

13 November 2026. This is a bounded factual-source assessment, not a blanket licence or legal sign-off for an international commercial database. Production publication still needs the owner's approval. No copied marketing text, company photographs, full portfolio tables or commercial-directory exports are released.

## NY3: minimum factual discovery record

**Narrow factual data review completed**, covering only facility identity, the company's reported operating relationship, source-qualified current facility status, American Tower's current common-equity listing, and the CC0 city reference. The basis is independently written individual facts, not permission to reproduce the source documents. US Copyright Office Compendium §313.3(C) and Feist distinguish facts from protected expression/creative compilation. The source is a US company's single named US facility announcement and current service page, corroborated by its parent's SEC disclosure. This is not a systematic substitute for the company's portfolio database.

Source-specific checks:

- The official NY3 launch announcement explicitly names the facility and operator; the current NY3 service page identifies the facility/address and adjacency to NY2. Only a short original factual record and links are selected.
- The discoverable CoreSite footer links to `/privacy`; the actual policy was checked, rather than treating guessed 404 terms pages as approval. That policy governs personal information. No express site-content commercial-use covenant was identified in the checked policy/footer. This finding is limited to those sources and is **not** proof no other agreement could exist.
- `robots.txt` permits the checked public article/facility paths; it does not grant a copyright licence. No login, account, payment, technological restriction or private system was used.
- The AMT SEC filing supports the parent/business chain. The common-equity identifiers are already an approved source category. Company ownership percentages are excluded from the facility-ownership field.
- The location uses Wikidata CC0, not the disputed official/OSM site pins. The source link/credit is retained.
- Capacity, area, price, marketing descriptions, photographs, diagrams and corporate marks are omitted. No table layout, expressive text, or database selection is republished.

The record-bound review in `field-reuse-review.json` is limited to that minimum factual projection. New fields, changed facts or expansion to bulk directory extraction invalidate the review and require a fresh source-specific assessment. If the publisher requires affirmative written permission even for this bounded US factual record, that remains a separate publisher-policy decision; this assessment is not such permission. Questions about database rights in other jurisdictions, contractual access, bulk copying and image reuse are deliberately not cleared.

## Geography and imagery

Wikidata structured geography: CC0, credited. Existing Energy geometry/verification: preserved with its original EIA/GLEIF/SEC provenance, not newly certified by Phase 4. OSM results: separate internal geolocation-comparison file only, ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors. No OSM coordinates enter the candidate release or the Globe. A future public OSM-derived layer must retain attribution and resolve applicable derivative-database obligations rather than silently mixing it into a closed export.

No Data Center photo or satellite image is cleared or used. Missing imagery does not prevent a clearly labelled city marker. Corporate photos are not freely reusable merely because downloadable. Project-specific Commons reuse requires identity plus file-specific author/licence/share-alike/crop compliance; none has passed this phase.

## Real Estate

Montea/WDP disclaimers limit material to personal/non-commercial use and reserve IP/database rights. Goodman terms restrict incorporation/distribution for commercial gain. SEGRO content/photo permissions must be scoped to the actual asset and use; a general media library is not permission to ingest a portfolio database. Real Estate extracts stay internal while field reuse, identity and security mapping are verified. Do not copy full directories or photographs.

Receipts in `sources/` and the previous phases identify exact URLs, retrieval times, hashes and access failures. Raw bodies remain ignored locally. A 404, HTTP 405 or script-only response is a source limitation, never a licence grant.
