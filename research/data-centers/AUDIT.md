## What is verified, and what is not

**26 official portfolio/business-affiliation records across 20 countries; zero independently verified property-ownership links.** A is explicitly limited to that relationship scope. Do not describe these as 26 direct project investments,26 separately verified construction projects or26 surveyed building sites.

Digital Realty's official global directory exposed 261 facility records; 29 records were sampled and deduplicated by provider + stable facility ID (25 Digital Realty, 3 Equinix, 1 CoreSite). Category A 26 (23 DLR + 3 EQIX), B 0, C 1, D 2. A means an official company record supports a specific facility's business-portfolio affiliation with the listed issuer. It does not establish land title, legal operator, percentage ownership, construction status, cash flows or direct project investment availability. Category B is fail-closed until a regulatory-chain adapter is reviewed. Category C CoreSite VA1 has an official facility page but only branding-level parent evidence; AMT parent/JV chain remains unverified. Category D DUB1/DUB10 retain the original contradictory UK country label for audit, excluded from preview and graph.

All 29 records are **not cleared for public release**. Financial relationship confidence, exact-site precision and reuse permission are independent gates. No shared verification rule or schema has been relaxed. Construction/operating status is unknown: directory publication is not commissioning evidence. Source publication dates remain null; retrieved dates are not substituted.

## Source and licensing review

See `licensing-assessment.json`, saved official terms/robots receipts and per-record evidence. Official domains fetched with `InvestediaRobot/1.0`; Digital Realty robots permits directory paths and prohibits taxonomy/node paths; those prohibited paths were not used. Equinix robots permits directory paths, disallows a content/dam path; no restricted image path downloaded. SEC requests were sequential and low volume. No paid API, model or authenticated service. Gemini calls0. No SEC bulk crawler.

Digital Realty [terms](https://www.digitalrealty.com/about/legal/terms) restrict copying/republication of site materials. Equinix [terms](https://www.equinix.com/about/legal/terms) similarly restrict copying/distribution absent authorization. Public accessibility is not a commercial redistribution grant. This is a bounded internal factual evidence study, not an approved source ingestion pipeline. Do not copy this directory, text or images into the public product before the source-specific reuse decision/permissions. **Zero commercial/public approvals.**

SEC agency listing facts and short issuer-authored cover evidence are distinguished from rights to reproduce whole issuer filings. DLR's cover response was bounded to its first 8 MB; only common-stock cover evidence is used, not a claim to audit the complete filing. Full corporate HTML/filings are ignored local research files. Committed `source-packets.json` retains minimal factual fields, cover-row evidence, source pointers, origin response hashes and its own integrity hashes for offline review. It is not an approved redistribution dataset.

OSM/Wikidata were not used as ownership proof. OSM was not downloaded; the prior owner ODbL approval requirement remains unresolved and share-alike obligations must be reviewed before importing it. No Commons photographs or satellite imagery were downloaded. Existing Natural Earth public-domain boundaries alone are copied for internal country screening/preview. No external scripts, image trackers, live market feeds or company imagery in the preview.

## Architecture and compatibility

Reused read-only: `data/registry.json` exact identities, SEC source/CIK conventions, `investedia.projects.v2` core field names, existing Natural Earth boundaries. Inspected existing P19 source/field-evidence licence gate and Claude's current GEM model without editing them. Plans/tracker/latest working brief make clear Energy, verification, imagery and live connections belong to Claude; they remain untouched.

`dataset.json` is an isolated research sidecar, **not a proposed change to shared Projects schema**. It separates A/B/C/D, role/relationship, exposure, listing evidence, licence state, geographical precision, original IDs and granularities. `projects-v2-research.json` uses existing fields only, `validation_status=research_only`, empty `own` and explicit uncleared licence. Existing public loaders reject it; it is not copied into assets/docs/ or production. No additional common schema fields are required for this proof of concept. The active Energy layer also uses Claude’s compact GEM-specific representation. No drop-in compatibility with that private adapter is asserted: final adapter/category and publishing decisions require a separate proposal/approval with Claude; the existing Energy schema is not changed here.

`exposure-graph.json` links stable facility IDs to existing exact registry asset IDs. Only `portfolio_association` /`indirect_corporate_equity` is allowed. Tests reject owner/developer/power/HQ/direct-investor substitutions, wrong IDs, preferred-stock targets, invented stakes and unbound evidence. No geographic-neighbor ownership or power relationship inference. Legal owners/operators/developers remain separate nulls rather than guessed parent-company names.

The local preview is a Projects workspace with Globe/Directory views, sector control, search/company filter, detail/source card and indirect corporate-exposure section. No separate live website page or production renderer modification. The sector control demonstrates Data Centers only; it does not load a fake Energy layer or invent another sector's records. Screenshots labelled on-image at 1440/390 in dark/light. Corporate logos/photos are not reused.
